How a flight starts

You carry the aircraft from the case to the pad yourself.

You start at the ute, in first person. Unfold the arms, take the gimbal cover off, walk back to the pilot station and tick the checks on the controller screen. Then the motors spin up and you have control.

01 · At the ute

Pick up the aircraft

The hard case sits on the tailgate. You open it and lift the aircraft out.

02 · Pilot station

Run the checks

Each line reads the real battery, compass, GPS lock, airspace and the people near the pad.

03 · Line of sight

Fly it from your own eyes

The aircraft is a speck against the sky. The camera feed is on the controller in your hands.

The job loop

A job runs from the quote to the invoice.

Each job follows the steps a commercial operator works through. You check the weather window and the airspace, write the risk assessment and brief the crew. Then you fly one or more batteries, process the footage and send it to a client who reacts.

Ten work sites across nine regions

From the Perth Hills to the Pilbara coast.

Each site is at real world scale, with the plants, light and weather of its region. The seasons change as well. The Wheatbelt goes from green crop to stubble, and the Murchison flowers after winter rain.

In the air

You fly each kind of job differently.

You frame a house for an agent, cover a paddock for a survey, hunt hot cells across a solar block, or thread a cinewhoop through a doorway while a spotter calls the gaps.

The hangar

Nine aircraft, from a 249 g mini to a heavy lift hex.

You can buy them, or hire by the day and keep your cash. Each has its own flight model, stepped at 500 Hz.

The company

You start with a borrowed ute and end up with a hangar.

The yard is your company's own block on the Swan Valley fringe, and you walk around it. It grows with what you buy. You trade the sea container store for a shed, then a hangar, and your crew park their cars there. After a bad job, your money and reputation recover. The weather can still upset the plan.

The Academy

Learn the real rules, then fly by them.

Training mode opens the career with lessons, practice quizzes and checkrides built on the real rules in three countries. The server replays every assessed flight from your inputs before it records a pass.
